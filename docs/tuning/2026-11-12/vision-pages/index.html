Why Frogs Matter

Environmental Indicators

Frogs are bioindicators, meaning their health reflects the health of their ecosystems. Their sensitive skin makes them vulnerable to pollution and environmental changes.

Pest Control

Frogs consume vast quantities of insects, including mosquitoes and agricultural pests, providing natural pest control worth billions of dollars annually.

Medical Research

Frog skin contains compounds with potential medical applications, including painkillers and antibiotics. They've contributed to breakthroughs in cloning and developmental biology.

What Makes Frogs Unique?

  • Amphibious Life: Most frogs live both in water (as tadpoles) and on land (as adults)
  • Remarkable Jumpers: Some frogs can leap over 20 times their body length
  • Breathing Through Skin: Frogs can absorb oxygen directly through their moist skin
  • Vocal Champions: Male frogs croak to attract mates, with some species heard up to a mile away
  • Vision Experts: Frogs have excellent night vision and can see in color
