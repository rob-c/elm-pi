# Scalar mediator toy model: what goes into the rates

## Fields and masses

- S: real scalar mediator, M_S = 600 GeV.
- a: real scalar (spin 0, its own antiparticle), m_a = 80 GeV.
- chi: Dirac fermion, m_chi = 150 GeV.

## Couplings

- kappa = 45 GeV (S a a), y = 0.12 (S chibar chi),
  Lambda = 2000 GeV (the chibar chi a a contact term).

## Squared matrix elements

Tree level, from the Feynman rules, with states normalised to 2E per unit
volume (ubar u = 2 m). Summed over final-state spins and averaged over
initial-state spins; nothing else is in them.

- S -> a a:            |M|^2 = kappa^2
- S -> chi chibar:     |M|^2 = 2 y^2 (M_S^2 - 4 m_chi^2)
- chi chibar -> a a:   |M|^2 = (s - 4 m_chi^2) / (2 Lambda^2)
  (the contact term alone; S exchange is left out at this energy)

S has no other decay channel.

## Constants

- hbar = 6.582119569e-25 GeV s
- (hbar c)^2 = 0.3893793721 GeV^2 mb
