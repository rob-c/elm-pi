# Far-detector muon-neutrino sample: truth-level cross-check of the fit inputs

- Beam: muon neutrinos, off-axis; far detector at a baseline of 295 km.
- unoscillated.csv: expected numu charged-current events in the far detector
  for the current exposure if there were no oscillations, in 10 MeV bins of
  true neutrino energy from 200 to 3000 MeV (flux x cross-section x
  efficiency, from the beam and detector simulation).
- For this cross-check: numu disappearance in the two-flavour approximation
  with theta23 and dm2_32, in vacuum. No theta13 correction, no matter effect,
  no energy smearing.
- Oscillation parameters, the best fit of our last analysis:
  sin^2(theta23) = 0.545, dm2_32 = 2.47e-03 eV^2.
- Analysis bins (true energy, MeV): 200-400, 400-600, 600-800, 800-1200, 1200-2000, 2000-3000.
