model.md has the fermion content of our U(1)_X model with two candidate charge assignments. For assignment A, work out the six anomaly coefficients defined there and say whether it is anomaly-free; for assignment B, find the value of the open charge z that cancels all six, and the U(1)_X charge the Higgs doublet then needs for the Yukawa terms in the note. Put it all in result.json: a_ccx, a_wwx, a_yyx, a_yxx, a_xxx, a_ggx (numbers, assignment A), anomaly_free_a (true or false), z_b and x_higgs_b.
